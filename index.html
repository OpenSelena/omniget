Core Capabilities

Designed to unify offline media extraction and study tools into one app.

Course Downloader & Player

Extract structured courses from Hotmart, Udemy, Kiwify, Gumroad, Teachable, Kajabi, and Skool. Watch offline with timestamped notes and attached PDFs side-by-side.

Global Hotkey Grabber

Copy any URL to your clipboard and press Ctrl+Shift+D. OmniGet fetches metadata and downloads the file silently in the background.

1,800+ Media Extractor

Powered by yt-dlp. Download video or audio from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Twitter/X, Reddit, Twitch, Vimeo, and 1,800+ web platforms.

Deep Bilibili VIP Support

Sign in to unlock official Bilibili premium streams: 4K, HDR, Dolby Vision, Hi-Res Lossless Audio, Danmaku subtitles, and Kodi/Jellyfin NFOs.

Built-in Reader Module

Organize and read your EPUB, PDF, CBZ comics, TXT, and HTML collection with cover fetching, paper themes, bookmarks, and highlights.

Hi-Fi Music Player & Lyrics

Local audio library player with scrolling synced lyrics. Integrates local tracks with Spotify, SoundCloud, YouTube Music, Qobuz, and Last.fm.
