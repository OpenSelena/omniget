Core Capabilities

Designed to unify offline media extraction and study tools into one app.

Course Downloader & Player

Extract structured courses from Hotmart, Udemy, Kiwify, Gumroad, Teachable, Kajabi, Skool, Wondrium, Thinkific, and Rocketseat. Watch offline with timestamped notes and attached PDFs.

Global Hotkey Grabber

Copy any URL to your clipboard and press Ctrl+Shift+D (or Cmd+Shift+D on macOS). OmniGet fetches metadata and downloads silently in the background.

1,800+ Media Extractor & Quality Picker

Powered by yt-dlp. Extract video/audio with real resolution selection (4K 2160p, 2K, 1080p, audio-only), SponsorBlock skipping, and auto-generated Whisper subtitles.

OpenOmni Social Archiver

Bulk extract posts, reels, stories, and galleries from Instagram, TikTok, Reddit subreddits/profiles, and Twitter/X profiles with integrated gallery-dl engine.

Telegram Direct Uploader & Bot

Upload media directly to Telegram via User Session or Bot Token with custom thumbnails, filename editing, 2GB/4GB auto-chunking, and Video/Doc/Audio modes.

Deep Bilibili VIP 4K Support

Sign in to unlock official Bilibili premium streams: 4K, HDR, Dolby Vision, Hi-Res Lossless Audio, Danmaku subtitles (XML/ASS), and Kodi/Jellyfin NFOs.

Built-in Reader Module

Organize and read your EPUB, PDF, CBZ comics, TXT, and HTML collection with cover fetching, paper themes, bookmarks, and highlights.

Hi-Fi Music Player & Lyrics

Local audio library player with scrolling synced lyrics. Integrates local tracks with Spotify, SoundCloud, YouTube Music, Qobuz, Last.fm, and Discord RPC.

Portable Mode & Zero Setup

Run directly from USB drives with portable.txt. System dependency detector auto-manages yt-dlp, FFmpeg, and PDFium binaries securely.
