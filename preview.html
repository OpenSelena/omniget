One app instead of five tabs.

You keep one site for Instagram, another for X, and a cheat sheet of yt-dlp flags because the commands never stick. OmniGet puts everything behind one clean interface.

Without OmniGet

You are the glue.

  • A different shady download website for every site, loaded with spam and ads
  • yt-dlp flags looked up again and again in terminal documentation
  • Exporting browser cookies manually just to download content you already bought
  • A stalled or interrupted download means starting over from zero percent

With OmniGet

Paste and move on.

  • One unified box for 1,800+ sites, online courses, torrents, and files
  • Format and resolution chosen from a visual menu, with exact yt-dlp flags preserved
  • Your browser session synced automatically via companion browser extension
  • Resume interrupted downloads, backoff on rate limits, and follow channels automatically

Built for every desktop

Rust and Tauri

OmniGet is written in Rust and renders using the native platform webview. It starts in under a second, consumes negligible RAM, and keeps your system snappy on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

Verified Binaries

Installs yt-dlp and FFmpeg automatically, updates them in the background, and verifies SHA-256 hashes before execution.

No Account Required

No sign-up form, no cloud account, no telemetry tracking. Settings and SQLite history live in your local data folder.

What you download stays on your computer.

OmniGet has no user accounts, no tracking servers, and no ads. Your history, settings, and session cookies stay in SQLite on your disk.

What OmniGet refuses to do

  • No telemetry. The app never reports what you download, when you open it, or what URLs you paste.
  • No ads, subscriptions, or account requirements. Free and open source under GPL-3.0.
  • No DRM cracking. DRM-encrypted course lectures and streams are detected and skipped.
  • No proxy middlemen. Downloads stream straight from the media host to your machine.

Questions people ask first

Is OmniGet really free?

Yes. OmniGet is 100% free and open source under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0). There are no paywalls, accounts, or advertisements.

How is it different from yt-dlp?

yt-dlp is the command-line engine OmniGet uses under the hood. OmniGet bundles and updates yt-dlp and FFmpeg, provides a visual queue with auto-retry, bridges private cookies from your browser, offers native extractors for Instagram and courses, and includes Study and Media Tools.

Can OmniGet download courses I purchased on Udemy or Hotmart?

Yes. The Courses plugin is statically embedded in the app. Log in with your account or use cookies from the extension to pick sections and download non-DRM lessons and attachments with automatic numbering.

Why does Windows SmartScreen or macOS Gatekeeper show a warning?

Because OmniGet is open source and built without an expensive corporate commercial signing certificate. On Windows, click "More info" and then "Run anyway". On macOS, run `xattr -cr /Applications/omniget.app` once.

Can AI agents use OmniGet?

Yes. OmniGet includes a built-in Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that gives AI tools like Claude Code, Cursor, and VS Code direct access to download, transcribe, and edit media on your system.
