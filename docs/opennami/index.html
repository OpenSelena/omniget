OpenNami Social Media Archiver

Download posts, stories, threads, and boards from Instagram, TikTok, X, and Pinterest at original quality, with request pacing that keeps your account under rate limits.

OmniGet OpenNami on macOS: Dedicated social media archiver with connected profile sync, media filters, and bulk downloader.

1. OpenNami Engine

OpenNami is OmniGet's built-in social media archiver (Rust, under src-tauri/src/commands/open_nami/). It drives gallery-dl and yt-dlp with your browser session, so files keep the quality the site serves, with no re-encoding and no watermark.

The download view: live progress, request pacing between requests, and an archive that skips posts you already saved.
  • Original files: Downloads come straight from the platform CDNs, so quality matches what the site serves.
  • Browser-like requests: Sends a Chrome desktop User-Agent and sec-ch-ua Client Hints, like a normal browser.
  • Archive skip-lists: Saves downloaded post IDs in archive_gallery-dl.sqlite3 and archive_yt-dlp.txt inside each download folder, so re-runs skip what you already have.
  • Per-platform pacing: Sleeps between requests per platform (TikTok: 0.6–1.2s, Facebook: 1.5–2.5s, Instagram: 2.0–2.8s, others: 0.8–1.5s) to stay under rate limits.

3. X / Twitter Toolkit (10 Tools)

Public X data comes through the FxTwitter API without login. Tools that need your account (bookmarks, follow audits, Grok) use your session cookies:

OmniGet X Tools: Unroll threads to Markdown, download maximum bitrate video, and export bookmarks.
  • Unroll Thread: Converts long, multi-tweet discussions into a clean Markdown or HTML document with all attached media preserved.
  • Post to PNG Card: Renders a tweet into a crisp, high-resolution PNG graphic card ready for sharing on social platforms.
  • Export Bookmarks: Backs up all your saved X bookmarks across folders into JSON, CSV, Markdown, or HTML.
  • Profile Media Archive: Downloads all high-resolution images, GIFs, and videos from an author's profile in one batch.

Dedicated downloaders for TikTok and Pinterest:

Pinterest Tools: Full board backups, original high-resolution pins, clean search without AI ads, and color palette extraction.
  • TikTok HD Video: Downloads original-quality MP4 video with no watermark, photo slide decks, and isolated background MP3 audio.

5. Rate Limiting & Account Protection Safeguards

To keep your account out of trouble, OpenNami paces and limits its own requests:

Safeguard Protocol Behavior & Mechanism Protection Benefit
Paced requests Sleeps a randomized interval between requests, tuned per platform (see the list above). Avoids the steady request rhythm that platforms flag as bot traffic.
429 backoff Stops for 10 seconds when a platform answers HTTP 429, then retries, up to 3 attempts. Lets a rate limit cool down instead of hammering the site.
Unfollow whitelist Protects pinned accounts and mutual follows from bulk unfollow suggestions. Your real connections are never unfollowed by accident.
Cookies stay local Session cookies are used only on your machine; nothing is uploaded to any server. No third-party service ever sees your login.
